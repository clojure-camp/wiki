- Intercepts
    - “Diagnose” why student is stuck, what they need
    - Give them the least amount possible to get them on their way
        - Ex. project idea
        - Ex. mention a function to look up
    - identify the student's "zone of proximal development"
        - wrt to a type of task, where are they at…
            - what can they do alone
            - what can they do with guidance <---
            - what can't they do
        - challenge with work within the "can with guidance" zone

- Zone of Proximal Development
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_of_proximal_development
    - progress
        - learner cannot do
        - learner can do with guidance
        - learner can do unaided
    - relevant:
        - more effective to provide “appropriate” content/exercise that’s at edge of current understanding
        - OK to aid a student (ex. just give the answer) as a pathway to them eventually doing it unaided

- Cognitive Load
    - depending on a learner’s capacity, it’s possible to overwhelm with “too much new stuff at the same time”
    - OK to focus on one area, then move on to others
